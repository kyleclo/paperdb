# Paper Statistics

- **Papers with fulltext body**: 21,662 (96.8%)

- **Main conference papers**: 18,804 (84.1%)
- **Workshop papers**: 3,567 (15.9%)

## Paper Length Distribution

## Generation


